# Coconnect commercial OS: intake questionnaire

_For Rik and his co-founder. Short answers are fine. "Don't know yet" is a useful answer too.
Where we already heard something on the call, it's written in italics. Please just confirm or correct it.
Anything you'd rather explain out loud, we'll cover in the daily context sessions._

---

## A. Business and offers
1. In one sentence each: what is a **core package**, and what is the **Hub**?
2. Core packages: what's the typical fee range, and what does the client get (scope, duration, prize pool)?
3. Hub: _stated as $1,000 plus 10% of the prize pool_. Is that right? What does a typical Hub client
   spend, and how often do they come back?
4. For one closed deal of each type, what does Coconnect actually earn? (Write it as a formula if you can.)
5. How many new clients a month would you like from this engine, per motion? _Stated capacity: 5 to 6 active._
6. Are there offers or sectors you want to **stop** selling to, or are about to start?

## B. Ideal client (ICP). Answer separately for core and Hub if they differ.
7. Which sectors? _From the call: prediction markets and iGaming respond best, "everyone else, not so much."_
   Rank the others you'd still take, such as DeFi, L1/L2, gaming, AI or memecoins.
8. Funding stage and raise size: minimum and maximum raise, and how recent it should be.
9. Token status: pre-TGE, TGE within N weeks, or post-TGE? Which is the sweet spot?
10. Geography or language limits, if any.
11. Company size or team signals, e.g. "has a Head of Marketing" or "at least N employees".
12. Give us **three signals of a great fit**.
13. Give us **three signals to walk away**, e.g. no token, no budget, or already working with a competitor.
14. Which current or past client is the perfect example, and why? _Outcome and Overtime?_
15. Which agencies are your competitors? Does a prospect already working with one of them rule it out?

## C. Who we contact
16. Which roles decide on a campaign? Rank them: CEO or founder, CMO, Head of Growth, BD, community lead.
17. Who do you **never** want contacted cold, e.g. investors or partners' portfolio companies?
18. Anyone we must exclude outright: existing clients, prospects already in talks, the market maker's portfolio,
    Bull Shark's lists? Can you send those lists?

## D. What has worked so far
19. Paste your **three best-performing outreach messages** (X DM, LinkedIn, Telegram, email). The ones
    that actually got a reply or a call.
20. Walk us through how Overtime went from the first message to a signed deal. Who messaged whom, on which
    channel, how many touches, and how long it took?
21. Which hooks get replies? _From the call: the Outcome campaign as proof, "we can replicate this for you."_ Any others?
22. Which clients can we name in outreach, and which must we never name?
23. Anything you've tried that clearly **didn't** work?

## E. Channels and senders
24. _Channel ranking from the call: X first, LinkedIn second, Telegram third (response rate low)._ Where does email sit?
    Do you have prospects' emails at all?
25. _For the Hub: X and Telegram, not LinkedIn._ Is that right?
26. Which **X accounts** can send? _About 5: Rik, co-founder, company, CMO, …_ List them with their handles.
    Who is allowed to reply from each account?
27. Which LinkedIn profiles can send, and which Telegram accounts?
28. Who on your side will review the weekly lead sheet (budget about 1 hour on Friday)?
29. Who will take over a conversation once a prospect replies? Your appointment setters, Rik, or someone else?

## F. Sales process
30. List your pipeline stages from first contact to paid.
31. For each stage: what must be **true** for a deal to sit there?
32. When do you send a proposal: on the first call or after it? What does a proposal look like today?
    Please send one example of each for core and Hub.
33. When is a deal lost? After how long without a reply?
34. Do you record sales calls? _Rik recorded our call._ Which tool do you use?

## G. Tools and access
35. Where does your team work day to day: Slack, Telegram, or something else? Where should lead sheets and alerts go?
36. Google Workspace or something else, for email and sheets?
37. Do you use any outreach, enrichment or scraping tools already?
38. Who will be the day-to-day **operator** of the system, with their own Claude subscription?
39. Do you have a GitHub account or organisation? We need one for the cloud routines.
