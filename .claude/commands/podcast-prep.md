Prepare a podcast episode for vAPI Network. Guest/topic: $ARGUMENTS

Read `vapi-network/03-podcast/podcast-playbook.md` first.

Research the guest (web search — cite sources) and produce:
1. **Guest brief** — who they are, what they've built, recent public positions, what makes them worth an hour
2. **The angle** — the one thing this episode is about that no other episode is
3. **12–15 questions**, ordered, opening easy and building. Include 2 questions likely to produce a genuinely quotable answer
4. **3 clip targets** — moments to steer the conversation toward, because we need them for LinkedIn
5. **Pre-call brief** to send the guest (short, one screen)
6. **The ask** — is this guest a potential vAPI user, supplier or referrer, and what's the natural follow-up

Save to `vapi-network/03-podcast/episodes/EP-NN-<guest-slug>/prep.md`.

Cite sources for every factual claim about the guest.
