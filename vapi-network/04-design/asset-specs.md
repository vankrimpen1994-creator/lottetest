# Asset Specs

## Dimensions
| Asset | Size | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Single image post | 1200 × 1500 (4:5) | 4:5 takes more vertical feed space than 1:1 — use it by default |
| Carousel (PDF) | 1080 × 1350 per page | 6–10 pages. Upload as PDF |
| Video clip | 1080 × 1350 (4:5) | 45–90s. Burned-in captions always |
| Quote card | 1200 × 1500 | Verbatim quote, attribution, watermark |
| Stat card | 1200 × 1500 | One number, dominant. Source line at the bottom |
| Podcast announce | 1200 × 1500 | Guest photo, name, title, episode title |
| Company banner | 1128 × 191 | Safe zone: keep text centred |
| Founder banner | 1584 × 396 | Right third obscured by the avatar on mobile |
| Document/lead magnet | A4 portrait | PDF, 8–16 pages |

## The 6 master templates (built in Week 0)
1. **Quote card** — big quote, small attribution, watermark
2. **Stat card** — one dominant number, one line of context, source
3. **Carousel cover** — title, subtitle, "swipe" affordance
4. **Carousel interior** — slide number, heading, ≤35 words
5. **Clip frame** — caption safe-zone, guest name lower-third, watermark
6. **Podcast announce** — guest photo, episode title, episode number

## Copy limits — enforce these
| Element | Max |
|---|---|
| Stat card context line | 12 words |
| Quote card | 30 words |
| Carousel cover title | 8 words |
| Carousel interior | 35 words |
| Any static image total | 25 words |

If the copy doesn't fit, **the copy is wrong** — cut it, don't shrink the type.

## Pre-delivery checklist
- [ ] "vAPI Network" watermark present and legible
- [ ] Contrast ≥ 4.5:1 on all text
- [ ] Legible at thumbnail (shrink to 15% and look)
- [ ] Source line on any asset carrying a statistic
- [ ] Correct dimensions and file format
- [ ] Carousel exported as PDF, not images
- [ ] Video captions burned in, not uploaded as a track
- [ ] Filename: `YYYY-MM-DD-<slug>-<type>.<ext>`
