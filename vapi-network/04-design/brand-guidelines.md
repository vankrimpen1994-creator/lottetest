# Brand Guidelines — working version

> `[NEEDS ASSETS]` Logo files, fonts and colour hexes to be supplied by the client.
> Everything below is the **working system** until then. Confirm on the handover call.

## The look, in one line
**Technical, restrained, high-contrast.** Closer to developer documentation than to a
crypto project. The visual job is to make "onchain" feel like infrastructure, not
speculation.

Reference points: Stripe docs, Linear, Vercel. **Not:** gradient meshes, glowing orbs,
3D coins, neon purple, rocket ships.

## Colour `[NEEDS CLIENT HEXES]`
| Role | Use |
|---|---|
| Primary | Headlines, key data points, the one thing the eye should hit first |
| Ink | Body text. Near-black, not pure black |
| Surface | Backgrounds. Near-white or near-black — pick one and be consistent |
| Accent | One only, used sparingly, for emphasis |
| Muted | Secondary text, labels, captions |

**Rule: one accent colour per asset.** Two accents reads as a slide from 2014.
**Contrast minimum 4.5:1** for all body text, verified per asset.

## Type `[NEEDS CLIENT FONTS]`
- **Display:** geometric or neo-grotesque sans. Tight tracking on headlines.
- **Body:** the same family, regular weight.
- **Mono:** for anything code, numeric or receipt-like. This is the signature move for
  a product about machine payments — use it deliberately.
- Maximum **two weights** per asset.

## Layout
- Generous margins. Empty space reads as confidence.
- Strict left alignment. Centred text only on quote cards.
- Text must be **legible at thumbnail size** — that's how most people first see it.
- One idea per asset. If it needs two, it's two assets.

## Watermark — mandatory
**"vAPI Network"** on every asset, bottom-left or bottom-right, muted colour, small but
readable. This is a direct mitigation for the `vapi.ai` name collision: assets get
screenshotted and reshared without the post, and the watermark is the only thing that
travels with them.

## Never
- Stock photos of people shaking hands, or anything from a "business" stock set
- 3D coins, tokens, rockets, moons, bulls
- Gradient-on-gradient
- More than 25 words on a static image
- Text over a busy photo
- Emoji as design elements
