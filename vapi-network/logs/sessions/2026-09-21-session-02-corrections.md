# Session 02 — Two corrections
**Date:** 2026-09-21 · **Goal:** correct the product story and re-aim the pitch at the agency

## Correction 1 — the proposition was pitched at the wrong altitude

Session 01 identified the right company (`vapinetwork.ai`, cited throughout
`client-profile.md`) but led the strategy with the **x402 payment plumbing and Call**
rather than the actual proposition: **hiring agents or humans for scoped work at a fixed
price, through USDC escrow.**

Confirmed via search: both sides sign the scope and price → buyer locks USDC in escrow →
accepted work releases payment → disputes go to three reviewers, two matching votes
release, refund or split.

**What changed:** Tasks is now the spine of the content plan, not a pre-launch footnote.
New rule across the project — *lead with the hiring problem, never the protocol; if a
hook's first two lines contain "x402", rewrite them.* Pillar one is now **How Work Gets
Commissioned** (30%). The x402/Linux Foundation material demotes to proof, mid-post.

Rewritten: `client-profile.md`, `icp-and-positioning.md`, `content-pillars.md`,
`voice-and-tone.md` (hook patterns), `CLAUDE.md` (hard rules), `90-DAY-PLAN.md`,
`SAMPLE-POSTS.md` (all three now open on a hiring problem), plus the slash commands,
post template, engagement examples and the monthly report template.

**Still open:** `vapinetwork.ai` is egress-blocked from this environment, so the site
couldn't be read directly. Is **Tasks publicly live** or in private beta? Flagged
`[UNVERIFIED]` in the profile and added to the discovery questions.

## Correction 2 — the meeting is with the agency, not the client

This reframes the pitch entirely. The agency is deciding whether to hand us content work
for their clients. They are not testing vAPI knowledge — they're testing whether we can
turn a client's goals into a plan and execute it reliably, unsupervised.

**What changed:**
- New `HOW-WE-WORK.md` — the five-stage production system (intake → strategy → production → QC → reporting), the transferability argument, the honest position on AI use, and white-label working terms. **This is now the primary pitch artefact.**
- `MEETING-RUNSHEET.md` → `AGENCY-RUNSHEET.md`, rebuilt: lead with the system, use the vAPI plan as the worked example. The name-collision finding demotes from opening gambit to *evidence that intake works*.
- `scope-and-pricing.md` → `agency-terms-and-pricing.md`: white-label by default, agency owns the client relationship, supplier terms, plus the multi-client economics argument.
- `objection-handling.md` rewritten for agency objections — consistency, factual risk, white-label, multi-client capacity, how much is AI.
- `discovery-questions.md` rewritten to ask the agency: is $1,300 monthly or total, **how many clients could this run for**, is VAPA the same shape.

## Carried forward unchanged
The founder-participation condition on the target bands, the kill criteria, the
no-invented-metrics rule, and the weekly operating rhythm.
