# Engagement Playbook

**A page with zero followers has zero distribution. Commenting is how you borrow
somebody else's.** This is half the programme and the half most agencies skip because
it doesn't produce a deliverable.

## The weekly numbers
- **Founders: 10 substantive comments per week each.** Not "great post" — 2+ sentences that add a data point, a distinction or a counter-example.
- **Company page: 5 comments per week** on ecosystem and partner posts.
- **Respond to every comment on our own posts within 2 hours.** The first 60 minutes after publishing is the highest-leverage hour of the week.

## Who to engage with

**Tier 1 — ecosystem amplifiers (ICP C).** x402 ecosystem builders, Base and Coinbase
devrel, MCP and agent-framework maintainers, AI-infra analysts and newsletter writers.
Biggest reach per comment, and this is the podcast guest pool. _Target: 8/week._

**Tier 2 — ICP A buyers.** Heads of platform, automation and AI leads, VPs of
engineering, agency owners posting about agents in production. _Target: 7/week._

**Tier 3 — ICP B supply.** API product owners, indie devs, data providers posting about
monetisation or billing pain. _Target: 5/week._

## What a good comment looks like
1. **Add a fact** — "x402 governance moved to the Linux Foundation in April with Visa and Mastercard as members, which changes the procurement conversation quite a bit."
2. **Make a distinction** — "Worth separating custodial from non-custodial here. Most agent-payment tools hold the funds; the risk profile is completely different."
3. **Offer a counter-example** — "We see the opposite in the x402 data: >80% settles on Base, almost all USDC."
4. **Ask a real question** — "How are you capping spend today? That's the part I never see a clean answer to."

## What never to do
- "Great post!" / "💯" / "This 👆"
- Pitching vAPI in a comment. Ever. The profile is the pitch.
- Correcting someone publicly in a way that embarrasses them
- Commenting on anything political, or on a competitor's bad news
- Copy-pasting the same comment across posts

## Connection requests
Send 15–20 per week, only to people whose post you've *already* commented on, with a
note referencing what they said. Never a pitch in the request.

## DMs — the rule
**Never open with a pitch.** Only DM someone who has engaged with us first, and always
reference the specific thing they engaged with. The M3 warm-DM playbook builds on this;
until then, DMs are for podcast invites and genuine replies only.

## Podcast invite = the best outreach we have
"Come on the podcast" gets a far higher reply rate than any pitch, because it's a gift
rather than an ask. Every week, 3 new invites to Tier 1 targets. Keep the guest pipeline
10 deep at all times.

## Log
Append weekly to `engagement-log.md`: who was engaged, what happened, anything that
turned into a conversation, new guest targets identified.
