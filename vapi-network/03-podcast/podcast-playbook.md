# Podcast Playbook

## Why it exists
Not for downloads. For three things, in this order:

1. **Content supply.** One 45-minute recording → 8–12 LinkedIn assets. It's the cheapest content we will ever make.
2. **Business development.** The invite list *is* the target list. "Come on the podcast" gets a reply where a cold DM gets deleted, because it's a gift rather than an ask.
3. **Borrowed authority.** A zero-follower page sitting next to a recognised ecosystem name inherits some of their credibility, and their repost reaches their audience.

Downloads are a lagging vanity metric here. Judge it on clips published, guest quality
and guest→network conversion.

## Format
- **45 minutes**, video + audio. Video is non-negotiable — it's where the clips come from.
- **Fortnightly** for months 1–2, weekly in month 3 if the pipeline supports it.
- One guest per episode. One clear angle per episode.
- Conversational, not interrogative. The host should disagree occasionally.
- Remote, recorded locally both ends (Riverside/Zencastr class tooling).

## Guest tiers
**Tier 1 — ecosystem builders (ICP C).** x402 projects, Base/Coinbase devrel, MCP and
agent-framework maintainers, AI-infra writers. Best reach, most likely to amplify.

**Tier 2 — practitioners (ICP A).** People running agents in production — agency owners,
platform leads, automation heads. **These episodes are sales calls that neither side has
to call a sales call.** Start booking these in month 3.

**Tier 3 — adjacent authority.** Security researchers, payments people, open-source
maintainers. Best for the Trust & Control pillar.

Keep the pipeline **10 deep at all times.** The best source of guest #7 is guest #5 —
always ask, on the recording, "who should we have on next?"

## The loop
```
target list (10 deep)
  → invite (reference their specific work; never a template)
  → 15-min pre-call (angle, clip targets, what they don't want asked)
  → record 45 min
  → edit + clip within 72h
  → guest reviews their clips before publish  ← builds goodwill, guarantees the repost
  → publish + 8–12 assets over 2 weeks
  → ask for the referral
```

## Per-episode asset set
| # | Asset |
|---|---|
| 1 | Episode announce + guest quote card |
| 2–5 | 4 × clips, 45–90s, captioned, 4:5 |
| 6 | Carousel — "N things [guest] taught us about X" |
| 7–8 | 2 × quote cards, verbatim only |
| 9 | Founder text post — the one thing that changed their mind |
| 10 | Repost copy handed to the guest |
| 11 | Audiogram / teaser |
| 12 | Newsletter / article version |

## Clip selection — what actually travels
- A strong opinion stated plainly
- A specific number with a story attached
- A disagreement, or a corrected assumption
- A concrete anecdote — "we lost $4,000 in an afternoon because..."
- **Never** the polite intro, the credentials recap, or anything needing setup to make sense

Hook in the **first 1.5 seconds**. Burned-in captions always — most of LinkedIn watches muted.

## Distribution
LinkedIn first (that's the brief). Then YouTube for search and embeds, then the audio
feeds. The guest's own repost matters more than any of them.

## Metrics
Downloads/episode · completion rate · **clips published per episode (target ≥8)** ·
guest→network conversion · guest referral rate · LinkedIn impressions from podcast-derived assets.
