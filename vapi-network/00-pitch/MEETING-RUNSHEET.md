# Meeting Run-Sheet — 30 Minutes

**Meeting:** vAPI Network trial · LinkedIn + podcast + design
**Goal of the call:** leave with the trial agreed, Week 0 starting, and one founder
committed to posting.

> **Open with the name problem. It is the single strongest credibility move available
> and it takes 90 seconds.** It proves we researched them, it's a real commercial
> problem, and we have the fix ready. Everything after that lands on a receptive room.

---

## 0:00–0:03 — Frame

> "We spent yesterday on your product, your GitHub and the x402 ecosystem. We're not
> going to pitch you generic social media. We found three things specific to vAPI that
> change how we'd approach this, and then a 90-day plan. Stop me whenever."

Do **not** open with agency credentials. Open with their problem.

---

## 0:03–0:09 — The three findings

**1. You have a name problem on LinkedIn specifically. (90 seconds)**
> "vapi.ai — the voice AI company, YC W21 — raised $50M in May and has over a million
> developers. On LinkedIn, search is name-match weighted. Type 'VAPI' and you get them,
> plus jobs in Vapi, Gujarat. You're invisible behind them."

Then immediately give the fix — never leave them sitting in the problem:
> "Free to fix, and we'd do it in week zero: the page is 'vAPI Network', never 'VAPI'.
> Tagline carries 'onchain task market' so link previews disambiguate. 'vAPI Network'
> in every post's first line and every image watermark. Own #x402, not #vapi. And claim
> the custom page URL this week before someone else does."

**2. Your entire competitive set is on Crypto Twitter. LinkedIn is empty.**
> "That's a gap, not an accident. CT rewards speed and memes. LinkedIn rewards
> credibility — and x402 is now governed by the Linux Foundation with Google, Visa,
> Mastercard, Stripe, AWS and Circle as members. That's a LinkedIn story. It's what
> turns 'crypto thing' into 'standard my board has heard of.' Nobody is telling it there."

**3. Your blocker is trust, not awareness — and you're unusually well armed for it.**
> "The objection is always the same: 'you want a bot to have my wallet?' Most
> competitors answer that with marketing. You answer it with mechanisms — spend policy
> applied *before* signing, key never leaves the machine, payments go direct, receipts
> to a local append-only ledger, Hacken audit, Apache 2.0. That's a whole content pillar
> and it's the one that converts enterprise readers."

---

## 0:09–0:19 — The 90-day plan _(screen-share `90-DAY-PLAN.md`)_

Keep it to the shape. Don't read the document.

- **You're a two-sided market, so the content is deliberately split.** Buyers — heads of platform, automation leads, agency owners. Sellers — API providers, dev shops, specialist freelancers. Fixed ratio, five pillars, so we never drift into all-product-all-the-time.
- **Week 0 is foundation.** Page, founder profiles, six design templates, 30 days of calendar approved in one batch so weeks 1–4 never wait on sign-off.
- **Month 1 — Foundation & Signal.** 28 posts. We're buying information, not leads. Find what the audience responds to.
- **Month 2 — Traction.** Rebalance around the data. Fortnightly podcast. First lead magnet: *"The Agent Spend Control Checklist"* — deliberately vendor-neutral so it travels.
- **Month 3 — Conversion.** Warm-DM playbook, proof content, weekly podcast, and a *"State of Agent Payments"* report.

**Then the numbers — deliver them exactly as bands, with the condition attached:**
> "From zero: month one, 300–600 followers and 8–15k impressions. Month two, 800–1,500
> and 30–60k, with 3–8 qualified conversations. Month three, 1,500–3,000 and 60–120k,
> 10–25 qualified conversations and 5–12 booked calls.
>
> Those are bands, not promises, and they hang on one thing I want to be direct about."

---

## 0:19–0:24 — The podcast engine + design

> "The podcast isn't a side project, it's the engine. One 45-minute recording becomes
> 8 to 12 LinkedIn assets — announce, four clips, a takeaway carousel, two quote cards,
> a founder post, the guest's own repost.
>
> And the invite list *is* the target list. 'Come on our podcast' gets a 30–40% reply
> rate from people who'd delete a cold DM. Six episodes in 90 days is six senior
> ecosystem relationships that started with us giving them something."

**Design:** six master templates in week 0, then 12–16 assets a month produced from
them, plus a weekly carousel and podcast art. Templated, not bespoke — that's how the
volume stays affordable. Full brand identity and motion are separate.

---

## 0:24–0:28 — The ask _(the part most pitches fumble — be direct)_

> "One condition matters more than everything else I've said. LinkedIn suppresses
> company-page reach. Personal profiles get five to twenty times the reach of a page
> with the same followers. If this is a company page posting alone from zero, we will
> not hit those bands, and I'd rather say that now than in month two.
>
> So: two founders or execs, posting twice a week, commenting ten times a week. We
> write everything, we prep it, we make it a 15-minute job. But it has to come from
> their profiles. Can we get that?"

Then the rest, quickly: 48-hour approval with one named approver · brand assets ·
1 founder hour a week · straight answers on what's actually shipping · CRM field for
LinkedIn-sourced leads.

**And the confidence move — offer the kill criteria yourself:**
> "One more thing, and it's in our interest to say it. If at week six engagement is
> still under 2% and qualified conversations are zero, we stop and re-plan rather than
> keep billing you for posts. We'd rather be measured than retained."

---

## 0:28–0:30 — Close

> "Week 0 can start Monday. First post goes live inside two weeks. What do you need
> from us to green-light it?"

Then **book the next thing before leaving the call** — a 30-minute brand/asset handover
and a founder profile session. A trial that doesn't have a next calendar entry dies.

---

## If you get 10 minutes instead of 30
Findings 1 and 3 → the two-sided split → the three monthly phases → the numbers with
the founder condition → the ask. Skip design and podcast detail; send the document.
