# vAPI Network — 90-Day LinkedIn & Podcast Plan

_Prepared 2026-09-20 · Trial engagement · LinkedIn from zero → B2B pipeline_

---

## The thesis in four lines

1. vAPI Network is a **two-sided market**. LinkedIn must recruit buyers *and* sellers, deliberately, on a fixed ratio.
2. **Every competitor in agentic payments is on Crypto Twitter. LinkedIn is empty** — and it's where the people who authorise spend actually are.
3. The blocker isn't awareness, it's **trust**. "Let a bot spend money" needs a mechanism answer, not a marketing answer. vAPI has the mechanisms — spend caps before signing, non-custodial keys, a local receipt ledger, a Hacken audit. Most rivals don't.
4. The **podcast is the engine, not a side project.** One 45-minute recording becomes 8–12 LinkedIn assets and puts a founder in a room with exactly the people we want on the network.

## Phase 0 — Week 0: Foundation (before a single post)

Unglamorous and non-negotiable. Skipping it is why most LinkedIn launches stall at week five.

**Fix the name problem first.** `vapi.ai` — voice AI, YC W21, $50M Series B in May 2026, 1M+ developers — owns every "VAPI" search on LinkedIn. vAPI Network is currently invisible behind it.
- Page name: **"vAPI Network"**, never "VAPI" alone
- Tagline carries "onchain task market" so link previews disambiguate
- Claim the custom page URL immediately
- "vAPI Network" in every post's first line and every image watermark
- Own `#x402` / `#agenticpayments` / `#agenteconomy`, never `#vapi`

**Also in Week 0:**
- Company page built: banner, logo, tagline, About (keyword-weighted for "x402", "agent payments", "AI agent infrastructure"), Products, custom CTA button
- **Founder/exec profile overhauls** — headline, banner, About, Featured section. This is where the reach comes from; the page is the credibility backstop
- Brand kit: 6 Canva/Figma templates (quote card, stat card, carousel cover, carousel interior, clip frame, podcast announce) in vAPI's colours
- Analytics baseline + tracking sheet; UTM convention agreed
- 30-day content calendar approved in one batch, so weeks 1–4 never wait on sign-off
- Podcast: name, cover art, format, first 10 guest targets, recording stack

**Deliverables:** optimised page, 2–3 optimised profiles, 6 design templates, 30-day calendar, podcast brand kit, tracking sheet.

## Month 1 — Foundation & Signal (Weeks 1–4)

**Goal:** publish consistently, establish the category voice, find out what the audience responds to. We are buying information this month, not leads.

**Cadence:** 4 company posts + 3 founder posts per week (28 posts total)

| Week | Focus | Notable |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Category education — "why agents need their own money rails" | Launch post; first carousel: *"x402 explained for people who don't do crypto"* |
| 2 | Trust & Control — spend caps, non-custodial keys, the receipt ledger | Poll: *"Would you let an autonomous agent spend from a company wallet?"* → doubles as ICP research |
| 3 | Build in Public — Call is live, what's real vs. roadmap | **Podcast pilot records.** First supply-side post: "monetise your API per request" |
| 4 | Proof & People — ecosystem, the Linux Foundation legitimacy story | **Episode 1 publishes** + 8 derived assets. Month-1 report |

**Engagement (the half that nobody budgets for and everyone needs):**
Founders comment on 10 ICP/ecosystem posts per week — substantive, 2+ sentences, adding something. A zero-follower page grows by borrowing audiences in other people's comment sections. Target list maintained in `02-linkedin/engagement-playbook.md`.

**Month 1 targets:** 300–600 followers · 8–15k impressions · 2–4% engagement · baseline established

## Month 2 — Traction & Amplification (Weeks 5–8)

**Goal:** double down on what month 1 proved, and start converting reach into contacts.

- **Rebalance the calendar around the data.** Whichever pillar carried month 1 goes to 35–40%; the weakest drops.
- **Podcast to fortnightly rhythm** — episodes 2 and 3. Guests drawn from ICP C (x402 ecosystem builders, Base/Coinbase devrel, MCP and agent-framework maintainers). Every guest amplifies to their own audience: that is the growth mechanism.
- **First lead magnet:** *"The Agent Spend Control Checklist — 12 questions to ask before you let an AI agent touch money."* Gated, email capture, promoted across 3 posts. Deliberately vendor-neutral so it travels.
- **Video scales up** to 2 clips/week from podcast footage. Captioned, 4:5, hook in the first 1.5 seconds.
- **Employee advocacy:** simple repost/comment pack to the wider team each Monday.
- **First partner cross-post** with an ecosystem project.
- **Supply-side push:** a dedicated week-7 sequence aimed at API providers and dev shops.

**Month 2 targets:** 800–1,500 followers · 30–60k impressions · 3–5% engagement · 3–8 qualified conversations · 50–150 emails

---

## Month 3 — Conversion & Compounding (Weeks 9–12)

**Goal:** turn an audience into pipeline, and hand over a system that runs without heroics.

- **Conversion layer:** warm-DM playbook (never cold-pitch; reference their comment or a post they engaged with), founder "open calendar" post, a clear route from post → first comment → booked call.
- **Proof content:** first user stories / integration walkthroughs — real numbers only, nothing invented.
- **Podcast to weekly** if guest pipeline supports it (episodes 4–6). Start inviting *buyer-side* guests: an agency owner or platform lead using agents in production. That conversation is a sales call disguised as an episode.
- **Flagship asset:** *"State of Agent Payments"* — a genuinely useful data-backed report. The thing that makes the page worth following rather than a thing to scroll past.
- **Second lead magnet** + retargeting of engaged viewers.
- **Day-90 retrospective:** what worked, what didn't, the honest numbers, and a 6-month plan with the case for scaling.

**Month 3 targets:** 1,500–3,000 followers · 60–120k impressions · 4–6% engagement · 10–25 qualified conversations · 5–12 booked calls

## The podcast engine — why it's the highest-leverage thing here

**One 45-minute recording → 8–12 LinkedIn assets:**

| # | Asset | Pillar |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Episode announce (guest quote card) | Proof & People |
| 2–5 | 4 × clips, 45–90s, captioned, 4:5 | Proof & People |
| 6 | Carousel: "6 things [guest] taught us about X" | Agent Economy |
| 7–8 | 2 × quote cards | Proof & People |
| 9 | Founder text post: the one thing that changed their mind | Build in Public |
| 10 | Guest's own repost to their audience | _borrowed reach_ |
| 11 | Audiogram / teaser | Proof & People |
| 12 | Newsletter / article version | Agent Economy |

**And it's a business-development channel.** The invite list *is* the target list. "Come on the podcast" gets a 30–40% reply rate from people who'd ignore a cold DM entirely. Six episodes in 90 days is six senior ecosystem relationships that started with us giving them something.

**Production loop:** Target list (10 deep, always) → invite → 15-min pre-call → record 45 min → edit → clip → publish → guest amplifies → next guest referred by the last one.

## Graphic design — what's included

Templated, not bespoke. That's how volume stays affordable.
- 6 master templates built in Week 0 (quote, stat, carousel cover, carousel interior, clip frame, podcast announce)
- ~12–16 static assets per month produced from them
- 1 carousel per week (6–10 slides)
- Podcast cover art + per-episode variants
- Video clip framing, captions, branding
- Lead-magnet layout (M2, M3)
- Page banner + founder profile banners

Out of scope at this tier: full brand identity, logo design, motion graphics, web design, print. Quotable separately.

## What we need from vAPI (the conditions, not the wishlist)

1. **≥2 founders/execs posting from personal profiles, 2×/week, commenting 10×/week.** The single biggest determinant of whether the target bands land. A company page alone, from zero, will not do it.
2. **48-hour content approval.** One named approver.
3. Brand assets — logo files, fonts, hexes.
4. Founder time: 1 hour/week (podcast + a content sync).
5. Product truth — what's shipping, when. We will not imply Tasks/Compute/Stake are live.
6. Analytics access + a CRM field for LinkedIn-sourced leads.

---

## How we report

- **Weekly, Mondays:** one screen. Top 3 posts, worst post + diagnosis, numbers vs. last week, what changes this week.
- **Monthly:** full dashboard, learnings, next month's calendar, one explicit ask.
- **Day 90:** retrospective + the case for what comes next.

**Kill criteria, agreed up front:** if at week 6 engagement is still below 2% and qualified conversations are zero, we stop and re-plan rather than keep shipping. Naming this in advance is what separates a strategy from a content subscription.
