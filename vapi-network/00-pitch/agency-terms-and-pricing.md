## Out of scope _(say it before they ask)_

Quotable separately: full brand identity or logo design · motion graphics · website
design or copy · paid ad management and spend · other channels (X, YouTube, Instagram,
newsletter) · outbound prospecting / SDR work · PR and events · community management
outside LinkedIn (Discord, Telegram) · studio hire, travel, in-person recording ·
translation.

**Fair-use guard:** rewrites caused by a change in client positioning *after* approval
are scoped separately. Not punitive — it just stops a client pivot silently eating a month.

## Making the price defensible

Don't defend the number. Reframe what it buys.

- **Per asset:** ~50 published assets a month at full scope ≈ **$26 per asset**, strategy, design and podcast production included.
- **Versus a hire:** a mid-level content marketer is $4–6k/month, can't produce a podcast, and takes a quarter to ramp.
- **Versus the agency's margin:** we're a cost line they mark up. The question isn't whether we're cheap — it's whether we're reliable enough to resell without supervision. **That's what `HOW-WE-WORK.md` is for.**

## If they push back on price
1. **Hold the price, narrow the scope.** Drop video clips or founder ghostwriting first — never cadence, never the podcast.
2. **30 days at reduced scope**, full scope from month 2 on agreed leading indicators.
3. **Bundle vAPI + VAPA** at a combined rate. Shared strategy overhead makes that genuinely cheaper to deliver, so the discount is real rather than conceded.

**Never discount by cutting posting frequency.** A programme posting twice a week instead
of four doesn't cost half as much to run and doesn't produce half the result — it
produces almost none.

## The multi-client question
If this works, the economics improve for both sides across more clients: the system,
templates, QC and reporting are already built, so client #2 costs less to set up than
client #1. **Worth raising at the close** — it reframes the trial as the start of a
pipeline rather than a one-off.
