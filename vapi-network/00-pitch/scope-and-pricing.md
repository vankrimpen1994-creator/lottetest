# Scope & Pricing

> **⚠ Assumption to confirm on the call.** The brief says "roughly $1,300 per project,"
> and VAPA is a separate project. This document assumes **$1,300 per month** for the
> vAPI Network engagement. If it's $1,300 for the whole 90 days, the scope below is
> roughly 3× too large — use the **Reduced Scope** column instead and say so plainly.
> **Settle this before talking deliverables.** Ask: _"Just so I scope this right — is
> the $1,300 monthly, or for the full trial?"_

## Explicitly out of scope _(say this before they ask)_

Quotable separately, at additional cost:
- Full brand identity or logo design
- Motion graphics / animated video
- Website design or copy
- Paid ad management and ad spend
- Other channels — X, YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, newsletter
- Outbound prospecting / SDR work (we generate inbound; we don't cold-DM at volume)
- Event, conference or PR support
- Community management outside LinkedIn (Discord, Telegram)
- Podcast studio hire, travel, or in-person recording
- Translation / localisation

**Fair-use guard:** two rounds of revision per asset are included. Rewrites caused by a
change in product direction or positioning after approval are scoped separately — not
punitively, just so a pivot doesn't silently eat the month.

---

## Making the price defensible

Don't defend the number — reframe what it buys.

- **Per asset:** ~50 published assets a month at full scope is roughly **$26 per asset**, strategy, design and podcast production included.
- **Versus a hire:** a mid-level content marketer is $4–6k/month, can't produce a podcast, and takes a quarter to ramp.
- **Versus the outcome:** one qualified enterprise conversation with a platform lead covers it several times over.

---

## What to propose if they push back on price

In order of preference:

1. **Hold the price, narrow the scope.** Drop video clips or founder ghostwriting first — never drop cadence or the podcast. Consistency is the thing that works.
2. **Trial period, then review.** 30 days at the reduced scope, full scope from month 2 on agreed leading indicators. Low risk for them, and the indicators are ours to hit.
3. **Bundle vAPI + VAPA** at a combined rate with shared strategy overhead. Genuinely cheaper to deliver, so the discount is real rather than conceded.

**Never** discount by cutting posting frequency. A programme that posts twice a week
instead of four times doesn't cost half as much to run and doesn't produce half the
result — it produces almost none.
