# Weekly Operating Rhythm

Roughly 6–8 hours a week at full scope. The discipline that matters: **draft a week
ahead, always.** Same-day drafting is how quality collapses and how approval delays
turn into gaps in the feed.

## Monday — Review & Engage
- [ ] `/report` → weekly snapshot. Top 3 posts, worst post + diagnosis, Tier 1 numbers vs. last week
- [ ] `/engage` → 20 target posts to comment on, drafted comments for the founders
- [ ] Send founders their comment pack + their 2 posts for the week
- [ ] Check DMs and comments from last week; escalate anything compliance-adjacent
- [ ] Post #1 of the week goes live

## Tuesday — Publish & Respond
- [ ] Post #2 live
- [ ] **First 60 minutes after posting are the highest-leverage time of the week** — reply to every comment
- [ ] Founder posts go live
- [ ] Log early performance

## Wednesday — Publish & Produce
- [ ] Post #3 live (usually the carousel)
- [ ] Podcast recording if scheduled, or `/podcast-prep` for the next one
- [ ] Outreach to 3 new guest targets

## Thursday — Next Week's Content
- [ ] `/weekly-content` → draft all 7 posts for next week
- [ ] Self-edit against `01-strategy/voice-and-tone.md` — especially the banned-words list
- [ ] **Send for approval. 48h clock starts.**
- [ ] Post #4 live

## Friday — Design & Repurpose
- [ ] `/design-brief` for each approved visual
- [ ] Produce graphics from the master templates
- [ ] `/podcast-repurpose` if an episode was recorded
- [ ] Schedule next week where approved
- [ ] Update the tracking sheet

## Monthly (last Friday)
- [ ] Full dashboard — Tier 1, 2 and 3
- [ ] Pillar performance review → rebalance next month's mix
- [ ] Refresh the guest pipeline to 10 deep
- [ ] Lead magnet production (M2+)
- [ ] Client call: results, learnings, next month, one explicit ask

## Approval flow
1. Draft → `02-linkedin/post-bank/` marked `STATUS: draft`
2. Batch of 7 sent Thursday → `STATUS: in-review`
3. Approved → `STATUS: approved`, design brief raised
4. Scheduled → `STATUS: scheduled` + date
5. Published → `STATUS: live` + URL, performance logged after 72h

**Unreturned after 48h = approved.** Agreed up front so the calendar can't be blocked
by silence.

## Escalation — never freelance on these
Route to the client, don't answer: anything about regulation, securities, custody,
tax or jurisdiction · claims about funds safety we can't source · competitor disputes ·
anything involving a named individual · press enquiries.

Answer ourselves, in public, with a mechanism: technical criticism, "how does this
differ from X", scepticism about agent spending. These are our best content.
