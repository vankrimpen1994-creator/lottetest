# KPI Framework & Target Bands

## Read this first — the honest version

These are **target bands from a standing start**, not guarantees. State them on the
call exactly as written. Promising a number you can't control is how trials die in
month two.

Two things drive whether the top or bottom of each band lands, and only one is in our control:

1. **Founder/exec participation on personal profiles.** LinkedIn suppresses company-page
   reach hard — personal profiles routinely get 5–20× the reach of a page with the same
   follower count. A company page alone, from zero, with no employee amplification, is
   the single most common reason a B2B LinkedIn programme fails. **This is the one thing
   we need from them, and it should be stated as a condition, not a request.**
2. **Approval turnaround.** Content that sits in review for a week loses its news hook.
   We need 48h.

## Metric tiers

### Tier 1 — Leading (weekly; tells us if it's working before revenue does)
| Metric | Why | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Impressions | Total reach | Page analytics + per-post |
| Engagement rate | `(reactions+comments+shares)/impressions`. **Target >3%** — below 2% the hook is wrong | Per post |
| Comments from ICP titles | The real signal. 5 comments from Heads of Platform > 50 from bots | Manual tag in tracker |
| Profile/page views | Intent proxy | Analytics |
| Follower growth + **follower quality** | % of new followers matching ICP A or B | Analytics demographics |
| Saves & shares | Highest-value signals LinkedIn rewards | Per post |

### Tier 2 — Lagging (monthly; what they'll actually judge us on)
| Metric | Definition |
|---|---|
| Inbound conversations | Someone starts a DM or comment thread about using vAPI |
| Qualified conversations | That person matches ICP A or B |
| Demo / call booked | Calendar event exists |
| Email captures | From lead magnets, M2 onward |
| Supply-side signups | API providers or task-takers who joined because of LinkedIn |
| Attributed pipeline | Tagged in their CRM as LinkedIn-sourced |

### Tier 3 — Podcast
Downloads per episode, completion rate, clips published per episode (**target ≥8**),
guest→network conversion (how many guests or their companies end up on vAPI),
guest referral rate.

## 90-day target bands

| | Month 1 | Month 2 | Month 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Followers (page, from 0) | 300–600 | 800–1,500 | 1,500–3,000 |
| Monthly impressions | 8k–15k | 30k–60k | 60k–120k |
| Avg engagement rate | 2–4% (baseline) | 3–5% | 4–6% |
| Posts published | 28 | 30 | 30 |
| Podcast episodes live | 1 (pilot) | 3 | 6 |
| Inbound qualified convos | 0–3 | 3–8 | 10–25 |
| Booked calls / demos | 0–1 | 2–5 | 5–12 |
| Email list | — | 50–150 | 200–500 |

**Assumptions these bands depend on — say them out loud:**
- ≥2 founder/exec personal profiles post 2×/week and comment 10×/week.
- 48h content approval.
- Podcast starts by Week 3 with ≥1 recognisable ecosystem guest.
- No paid spend. (Bands are organic-only. £/$500–1,000/mo of Thought Leader Ads on
  the best-performing founder posts would roughly double the month-3 reach band —
  flag as an option, not a requirement.)

## What we report and when
- **Weekly (Mondays):** one-screen snapshot — top 3 posts, bottom 1 with the diagnosis,
  Tier 1 numbers vs. last week, what we're changing this week. Template in `05-ops/reporting/`.
- **Monthly:** full dashboard, Tier 1 + 2 + 3, what we learned, next month's plan,
  one explicit ask.
- **Day 90:** the retrospective and the renewal case.

## Kill criteria (build this into the trial — it makes us look confident)
If after 6 weeks engagement rate is still under 2% and qualified conversations are
zero, we stop and re-plan rather than keep shipping posts. Named in advance, this
reads as rigour. Unnamed, a bad month reads as failure.
