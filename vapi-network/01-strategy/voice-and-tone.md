# Voice & Tone

## The voice in one line
**A senior engineer explaining something they genuinely find interesting, to a smart
person who doesn't work in crypto.**

Calm, specific, technically literate, allergic to hype. The credibility comes from
precision, not enthusiasm.

## Rules

**Do**
- Short sentences. Concrete nouns. Real numbers with sources.
- Lead with the problem the reader already has, not the product.
- Say the mechanism: "spend policy is applied before signing" beats "secure by design".
- Admit what isn't built yet. Pre-launch honesty is a trust asset for this ICP.
- Use "you" and "we". Never "one" or the passive voice.

**Don't**
- No emoji as bullets. One in a sign-off at most.
- No "🚀", "gm", "wagmi", "ser", "bullish", "wen", or any token/price talk.
- No "excited to announce" / "thrilled to share" / "game-changer" / "revolutionary" / "unlock".
- No broetry (one-line paragraphs stacked for drama).
- No engagement bait. No "comment X and I'll DM you" until we've earned the audience.
- Never say "crypto" where "stablecoin settlement" or "programmable payments" is accurate.
- Never "VAPI" alone. Always "vAPI Network".

## Two registers

**Company page** — institutional, precise, slightly formal. Explains the category.
This is the register that survives a compliance reader.

**Founder profile** — first person, opinionated, shows the work. Allowed to be blunt
("most agent-payment tools are custodial and nobody says so out loud"). This is where
reach actually comes from.

## The first rule of every hook
**Start at the hiring problem, not the protocol.** If the first two lines contain "x402",
"onchain" or "non-custodial", rewrite them. The plumbing is proof, and proof belongs in
the middle of a post.

## Hook patterns that work for this ICP
1. **The lived scene** — "The contractor went quiet at 80% done."
2. **The reversal** — "The money was already locked before you wrote the first line."
3. **The uncomfortable question** — "What's gone wrong most often when you've hired someone?"
4. **The correction** — "x402 isn't a crypto project. Governance sits with the Linux Foundation, and Visa and Mastercard are members." _(good mid-post, weak as an opener)_
5. **The specific number** — "165 million agent payments happened last year. Almost none had a spend cap."

## Banned words
excited, thrilled, delighted, game-changer, revolutionary, disrupt, unlock, leverage,
seamless, robust, cutting-edge, synergy, best-in-class, paradigm, journey, ecosystem
(as filler), "the future of X is here".
