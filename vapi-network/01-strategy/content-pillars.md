# Content Pillars

Five pillars. Every post maps to exactly one. Fixed weekly mix so the feed stays
balanced and we never drift into all-product-all-the-time.

**The rebalance that matters:** the biggest pillar is now *How Work Gets Commissioned* —
the hiring and escrow story — not protocol education. The protocol is proof, not the pitch.

## Pillar detail

### 1. How Work Gets Commissioned
The Tasks flow explained from every angle: scoping work so "done" isn't arguable;
why fixed price beats hourly for defined deliverables; what escrow actually changes for
both sides; the three-reviewer dispute process; what happens when work is rejected.

_Angle that works:_ start from a hiring story everyone recognises — the contractor who
went quiet, the invoice at day 70 — then show the mechanism that removes it.

### 2. Trust & Control
Counterparty risk on both sides. Money held, not sent. Review before release.
Non-custodial key handling. Spend caps applied before signing. The local receipt ledger.
Hacken audit. Apache 2.0.

_Angle that works:_ name the fear out loud, then answer it with a mechanism. This is the
pillar that converts sceptical buyers, and where most competitors are weakest.

### 3. The Agent Economy
Agents that commission work, not just consume APIs. Why "without accounts, dashboards or
manual workflows" is the real unlock. x402 under Linux Foundation governance — Google,
Visa, Mastercard, Stripe, AWS, Circle. Market data: 69,000 active agents, 165M
transactions, >80% on Base.

### 4. Proof & People
Podcast announcements, clips, quote cards, takeaway carousels. Ecosystem spotlights.
Real user stories when we're cleared to tell them.

### 5. Supply-Side Pitch
"The money was locked before you started." No invoice chasing, no 60-day terms,
no platform owning your client. How to list a service or take a task.

## Hard rules
- **Lead with the hiring problem, not the protocol.** If the first two lines contain "x402", rewrite them.
- One idea per post. If it needs two, it's two posts.
- Hook in the first 2 lines — mobile truncates at ~3.
- No links in the body. First comment, always.
- 3–5 hashtags at the end. Never `#vapi`.
- Every claim sourced. No invented numbers, quotes or case studies.
- Never claim ship dates for Compute or Stake.
