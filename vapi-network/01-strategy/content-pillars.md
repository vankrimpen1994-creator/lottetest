# Content Pillars

Five pillars. Every post maps to exactly one. The weekly mix is fixed so the feed
stays balanced and we never drift into all-product-all-the-time.

## Weekly mix (4 company posts + 3 founder posts per week)

| # | Pillar | Share | Job it does | ICP |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The Agent Economy** | 30% | Category education. Make "agents that pay" normal and inevitable. | A + B |
| 2 | **Trust & Control** | 25% | Kill the "you want a bot to have my wallet?" objection before it's raised. | A |
| 3 | **Build in Public** | 20% | Roadmap, design decisions, what shipped, what broke. Earns dev credibility. | B + C |
| 4 | **Proof & People** | 15% | Podcast clips, guest insight, ecosystem voices, integrations. Borrowed authority. | All |
| 5 | **Supply-Side Pitch** | 10% | Direct "list your API / take tasks" recruitment. | B |

## Pillar detail

### 1. The Agent Economy
Why agents need their own money rails; what breaks when they don't have them;
x402 explained without jargon; the Linux Foundation / Visa / Mastercard legitimacy
story; MCP + payments; market data (69k active agents, 165M transactions, >80% on Base).

_Angle that works:_ "Your AI agent has a credit card problem." Start at the pain,
not the protocol.

### 2. Trust & Control
Spend caps before signing. Non-custodial key handling. The local append-only ledger.
Hacken audit. Apache 2.0. Threat modelling. "What happens if the agent goes rogue."

_Angle that works:_ Name the fear out loud, then answer it with a mechanism. This is
the pillar that converts sceptical enterprise readers, and it's where most crypto
companies are weakest.

### 3. Build in Public
What shipped this week. Why a design decision went the way it did. The gateway daemon,
per-key budgets, OpenTelemetry tracing. Honest "this is on the roadmap, not live yet"
posts about Tasks / Compute / Stake.

_Angle that works:_ Specific and slightly unflattering beats polished. "We rewrote the
spend-policy layer because the first version checked the cap *after* signing" is a
better post than "excited to announce."

### 4. Proof & People
Podcast episode announcements, clips, quote cards, takeaway carousels. Ecosystem
partner spotlights. Integration walkthroughs. Community contributions.

### 5. Supply-Side Pitch
"Monetise your API per request, no billing page." "Get paid in USDC, no invoice."
Walkthrough of listing a service. Earnings mechanics. Discovery via x402scan / Bazaar.

## Content formats and how often

| Format | Cadence | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Text + single image | 3×/wk | Highest reliability, fastest to produce |
| Carousel (PDF, 6–10 slides) | 1×/wk | Best dwell time on LinkedIn; the workhorse for education |
| Video clip (0:45–1:30, captioned, 1:1 or 4:5) | 1–2×/wk | Podcast-derived, zero marginal cost |
| Text-only founder post | 2×/wk | Founder profiles outperform pages; no asset needed |
| Document/report (lead magnet) | 1× per month, from M2 | Converts reach → email → conversation |
| Poll | 1× per fortnight | Cheap reach + genuine ICP research |

## Hard rules
- One idea per post. If it needs two, it's two posts.
- Hook in the first 2 lines — LinkedIn truncates at ~3 lines on mobile before "see more".
- No outbound links in the post body. Link goes in the first comment, always.
- 3–5 hashtags, at the end. Never `#vapi`.
- Every post ends with a question or a concrete next step, never "thoughts?".
- Claims get sources. If we can't source it, we don't post it.
