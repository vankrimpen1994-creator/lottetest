## 4. What Alex proposed
A whole commercial engine, not just a lead list:
1. **Lead generation routine:** a weekly scheduled batch built from ICP criteria (recently funded,
   sector, heading to TGE, etc.) drawn from fundraising and ICO databases, with results posted to
   Slack as a sheet plus a ready-to-import CRM file.
2. **Enrichment:** decision makers (CEO, Head of Marketing), with their X, LinkedIn, Telegram and email.
3. **CRM:** leads, stages, meetings booked.
4. **Multichannel outreach:** sequences that fall back to the next channel when one is missing.
   Order to be set by the channel ranking above.
5. **Personalisation:** built from public signals (e.g. volume the prospect has announced) plus our own case-study hook.
6. **Dashboard:** leads generated, meetings booked, pipeline.
7. **Call intelligence:** Fireflies call context feeding the CRM.
8. **Automated proposals** generated from call context.
9. **Two separate motions:** (a) core packages, (b) Hub.
